How should the words "breathe"?

Since we're doing a per-word blur, the pacing determines the emotional impact.

A

Steady Pulse

Every word follows a constant beat (e.g., every 0.3s). Very consistent and rhythmic.

Four years ago... we walked in...
B

Expressive (Punctuation-Aware)

Short pauses between words, but a longer "breath" at commas, ellipses (...), and line breaks. Feels more like a spoken story.

Four years ago... we walked in...
C

Phrasal Reveal

Reveals small groups of 2-3 words at a time. Less "busy" than per-word, emphasizing key thoughts.

Four years ago... we walked in as strangers.
