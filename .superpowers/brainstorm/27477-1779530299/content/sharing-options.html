Refining the Sharing Experience

How should classmates see each others' memories?

A

The Graduation Wall

A public feed within the app where everyone's immediate photos (not the letters!) appear. Anyone with the link can scroll and see the class of 2026.

B

Private Direct Sharing

Users get a unique link to their specific photo or gallery that they can text/DM to friends. No central public feed.

C

Standard Social Share

A "Share" button that opens the phone's native share sheet (to post on IG stories, WhatsApp, etc.).
