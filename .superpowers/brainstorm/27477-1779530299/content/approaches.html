Proposed Approaches

Choosing the right technical and moderation path

1. The Curated Gallery (Recommended)

Moderation: You get a hidden "Admin Dashboard" to toggle approval for the Wall.

Sharing: Generates a dynamic "Story Card" (Spotify-style) using an HTML canvas for perfect Instagram layout.

Capsule: Database-locked until the unlock date. Email reminder sent automatically.

PRO: High quality, full control, easiest to manage.

2. The Lightweight Artifact

Moderation: Pre-filtering by a simple AI (to avoid blur/junk) before you do a final pass.

Sharing: Focuses on the Phone's native "Share Sheet". Link previews look great in WhatsApp/iMessage.

Capsule: Simple timer-based unlock on the site.

PRO: Very fast to build, minimal maintenance.
